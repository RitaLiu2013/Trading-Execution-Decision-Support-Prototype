Trading execution costs can include both explicit and implicit costs.

Explicit costs are direct trading costs such as commissions, transaction taxes, exchange fees, and similar charges.

Implicit trading costs can include the bid-ask spread, market impact, delay costs, and costs related to trades that are not completed.

Implementation shortfall is a framework for measuring the total cost of implementing an investment decision.

It compares the result of actual trading with a hypothetical portfolio in which the investment decision could be implemented at the price prevailing when the decision was made.

The price prevailing around the time of the trading decision is often referred to as a decision price or arrival price.

VWAP stands for Volume-Weighted Average Price.

A VWAP-based transaction-cost estimate compares execution prices with average market prices during an execution period.

Different execution benchmarks answer different questions, so no single benchmark should automatically be treated as appropriate for every trading objective.
